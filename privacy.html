AI News Summarizer: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Who uses this application

AI News Summarizer is a private application used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public, and no other person's Google account is connected to it.

Google data the application accesses

  • YouTube (read-only): the list of videos in one playlist the owner designates, including video IDs, titles, channel names, descriptions and publication dates.
  • Gmail (read-only): newsletter emails from approved senders in the owner's inboxes, used as news source material.
  • Google Drive (files created by the app only): the application uploads a daily copy of its collected source material to a folder in the owner's Drive. It cannot see or change any other Drive files.
  • Basic profile (email address): used only to confirm that access was granted by the owner's account.

The application never creates, changes or deletes YouTube content or email.

How the data is used

The data is used only to produce the owner's private AI-news briefing. It is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone else. Excerpts of collected source material may be sent to an AI model provider (such as OpenAI or Google Gemini) to rank and summarize the news; those providers process it only to return the result.

Storage and retention

Collected material and briefings are stored in the owner's private Google Cloud project and Google Drive. Access tokens are stored as secrets in that private project. The owner may delete stored material at any time.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections.

Google API Services User Data Policy

AI News Summarizer's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised by opening an issue on GitHub.
