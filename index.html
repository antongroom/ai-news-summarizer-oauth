AI News Summarizer

AI News Summarizer is a private, single-user application that prepares a personal weekly briefing on artificial-intelligence news.

Once a day it collects source material the owner has chosen: AI newsletters in the owner's Gmail inboxes, videos the owner adds to a designated YouTube playlist, and selected public sources. It then ranks and summarizes that material into a briefing, and saves a copy of each day's source material to a folder in the owner's Google Drive.

The application is not offered to the public and has no other users.
